The Rise of GloRilla

The Meeting That Changed Everything

In 2021, GloRilla connected with Memphis beatmaker Hitkidd at a local rap showcase. The two joined forces on a collaborative EP called Set The Tone, which featured five female Memphis MCs. It was the beginning of a creative partnership that would soon change both of their lives. Hitkidd had a beat he had originally lined up for Megan Thee Stallion. When that didn't work out, he brought it to Glo.

F.N.F. and the Breakthrough (2022)

In the spring of 2022, GloRilla recorded "F.N.F. (Let's Go)" with Hitkidd — a stark, piano-driven crunk anthem about independence and freedom. The track exploded on TikTok, with users across the country recreating its most iconic visual: twerking at a red light. The song climbed into the top 50 of the Billboard Hot 100, and within months, GloRilla was everywhere. "F.N.F." was later nominated for Best Rap Performance at the 65th Annual Grammy Awards, cementing her arrival.

Signing with CMG

The viral success of "F.N.F." caught the attention of Yo Gotti, one of Memphis's most powerful rap figures and the founder of Collective Music Group (CMG). GloRilla signed with the label in July 2022, joining a roster that includes Moneybagg Yo. It was a pivotal move that gave her the resources and platform to scale her career without losing her Memphis roots.

Building the Catalog (2022-2023)

After the signing, GloRilla didn't slow down. Her single "Tomorrow" was reworked into "Tomorrow 2" featuring Cardi B — her cousin — and became her first top-ten hit on the Billboard Hot 100, earning double platinum certification. She also released the gold-certified "Blessed." All three songs preceded her debut EP, Anyways, Life's Great, which dropped in November 2022 and debuted at number 11 on the Billboard 200. She won Best New Hip-Hop Artist at the 2022 BET Hip Hop Awards and a similar honor at the 2023 iHeartRadio Music Awards. In June 2023, she was selected for the prestigious XXL Freshman Class.

Going Commercial (2024)

GloRilla made 2024 one of the biggest years of her career. She dropped the high-energy single "Yeah Glo!" and the Megan Thee Stallion collaboration "Wanna Be," both of which cracked the Billboard Hot 100 top 40. Her debut commercial mixtape, Ehhthang Ehhthang, arrived in April 2024 and peaked at number 18 on the Billboard 200. Later that year came "TGIF," which set the stage for the main event: her debut studio album Glorious, released in October 2024. The album debuted at number five on the Billboard 200, achieving the highest first-week sales of any female rapper's album that year. She performed at the 2024 MTV Video Music Awards and appeared on Tyler, the Creator's album Chromakopia on thetrack "Sticky."

The Glorious Tour (2025)

GloRilla supported Glorious with a headline tour that ran from March through July 2025, closing out with a hometown show in Memphis. The Glorious Tour marked a new chapter — proof that the girl who once nearly lost everything had become a bona fide headliner.
