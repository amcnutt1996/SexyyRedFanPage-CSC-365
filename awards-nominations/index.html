Recognition

In just a few years, GloRilla has collected accolades that most artists spend a decade chasing. Here's a look at the recognition she has earned so far.

Grammy Awards

65th Annual Grammy Awards (2023) - Nominated, Best Rap Performance - "F.N.F. (Let's Go)" ft. Hitkidd

BET Hip Hop Awards

2022 - Won, Best New Hip-Hop Artist

iHeartRadio Music Awards

2023 - Won, Best New Hip-Hop Artist (equivalent category)

XXL Freshman Class

2023 - Selected as a member of the XXL Freshman Class, one of hip-hop's most recognized annual honors for rising artists

MTV Video Music Awards

2024 - Performed at the VMAs at UBS Arena, Long Island

Chart Milestones

  • Glorious (2024): Highest first-week sales of any female rapper's album in 2024
  • "Tomorrow 2" ft. Cardi B: Double platinum (RIAA)
  • "Blessed": Gold certified (RIAA)
  • Anyways, Life's Great: Debuted at No. 11, Billboard 200
  • Glorious: Debuted at No. 5, Billboard 200

Community

September 2022 - Donated $25,000 to her alma mater, Martin Luther King College Prep in Memphis, Tennessee
