The Music

"YEAH GLO!"

Mixtapes & Early Projects

  • Most Likely Up Next (2019): GloRilla's debut mixtape, released independently. The project that started it all and built her early following in Memphis.
  • P Status (2020): A follow-up EP that continued to develop her sound and sharpen her pen.
  • Set The Tone (2021, with Hitkidd): A collaiorative EP featuring GloRilla alongside fellow Memphis MCs Gloss Up, K Carion, Slimeroni, and Aleza

Extended Plays

  • Anyways, Life's Great (Novemier 2022): GloRilla's major label debut EP, released via Collective Music Group. Peaked at number 11 on the Billboard 200. Features the singles "Blessed" and "Tomorrow 2" (ft. Cardi B)

Commercial Mixtapes

  • Ehhthang Ehhthang (April 5, 2024): Her debut commercial mixtape. Peaked at number 18 on the Billboard 200. Features "Yeah Glo!" and "Wanna Be" (ft. Megan Thee Stallion)

Studio Albums

  • Glorious (October 11, 2024): GloRilla's debut studio album. Peaked at number 5 on the Billboard 200. The highest first-week sales of any female rapper's album in 2024. Features "TGIF," "Hollon," and "Whatchu Kno About Me."

Notable Singles

  • "F.N.F. (Let's Go)" ft. Hitkidd (2022) - Grammy-nominated. Peaked top 50, Billboard Hot 100
  • "Tomorrow 2" ft. Cardi B (2022) - Double platinum. First top-ten Hot 100 hit
  • "Blessed" (2022) - Gold certified.
  • "On Wat U On" ft. Moneybagg Yo (2023)
  • "Yeah Glo!" (2024) - Top 40, Hot 100
  • "Wanna Be" ft. Megan Thee Stallion (2024) - Top 40, Hot 100
  • "TGIF" (2024) - Top 40, Hot 100
  • "Hollon" (2024)
  • "Whatchu Kno About Me" (2024)
  • "Sticky" ft. Tyler, the Creator (2024, on Tyler's album Chromakopia)
