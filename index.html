Who is Glo Rilla?

GloRilla is one of the most explosive voices in hip-hop today. Born Gloria Hallelujah Woods in Memphis, Tennessee, she went from working a drive-thru and nearly going broke to landing Grammy nominations, platinum records, and a debut album that cracked the Billboard 200 top five — all within a few years. With her deep, commanding drawl and unapologetically bold style, Glo doesn't just make music. She makes moments.
